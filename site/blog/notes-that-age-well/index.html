Writing Technical Notes That Age Well

Published 2026-01-23 · 2 min read

Technical notes decay when they record only the current shape of a system. A durable note preserves enough reasoning for a later reader to decide whether the old conclusion still applies.

Record the decision boundary

Include the constraints that mattered, the alternatives seriously considered, and the evidence available at the time. Avoid pretending that a decision was inevitable.

Say what would cause the team to reconsider. “Revisit when writes exceed 500 per second” is more actionable than “this may not scale.”

Prefer links with summaries

External sources disappear or change. Link to them, but add a sentence explaining the fact or argument the link supports. The note should remain intelligible when a reference is unavailable.

Date meaningful revisions

An updated date tells the reader that someone rechecked the note. A short revision line can distinguish a factual correction from a changed recommendation.
